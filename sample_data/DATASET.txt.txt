SKYWATCH - DATASETS USED (Team Cipher, CipherCop 2026, Track 1)

1. Sample aerial surveillance images (included in this folder)
   Synthetic/generated aerial test images used to demonstrate the AI detection
   and geotagging pipeline. Generated by backend/app/db/generate_samples.py.

2. Simulated drone telemetry and geofences (generated at runtime)
   Drone positions, battery, altitude, signal strength, geofence polygons and
   demo incidents (Hyderabad area) are simulated and seeded into SQLite by
   backend/app/db/seed.py. No real or personal data is used.

3. Pretrained model: YOLOv8n (Ultralytics)
   Weights file: yolov8n.pt (included in the source code zip).
   Pretrained on the COCO dataset (80 classes incl. person, car, truck, boat).
   COCO: https://cocodataset.org
   Ultralytics COCO docs: https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/detect/coco/

4. Map tiles (loaded online at runtime, not stored)
   OpenStreetMap and Esri World Imagery basemap tiles.

Note: No personal data or real faces are used anywhere in this project.
